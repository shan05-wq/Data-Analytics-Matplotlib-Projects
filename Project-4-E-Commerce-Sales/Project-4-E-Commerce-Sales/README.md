# Project 4 - E-Commerce Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project uses Python and Matplotlib to analyze and visualize e-commerce sales data.

The project explores monthly sales, profit, order volume, product performance, and the relationship between sales and profit.

A complete dashboard is also created by combining multiple visualizations into a single figure.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- Matplotlib

## 📊 Visualizations

The project includes:

- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Product
- Monthly Profit
- Order Volume Distribution
- Sales vs Profit
- Product Contribution
- Complete E-Commerce Dashboard

## 📈 Analysis

### Monthly Sales Trend

A line chart is used to visualize how sales change throughout the year.

The highest sales point is highlighted using an annotation.

### Sales by Product

A horizontal or vertical bar chart is used to compare sales across different products.

Values are displayed next to the bars for easier interpretation.

### Monthly Profit

A bar chart is used to compare monthly profit values.

### Order Volume Distribution

A histogram is used to understand the distribution of monthly order volumes.

### Sales vs Profit

A scatter plot is used to explore the relationship between sales and profit.

### Product Contribution

A pie chart shows each product's contribution to total sales.

### E-Commerce Dashboard

A 2×3 subplot layout combines six visualizations into a single dashboard.

## 🔍 Key Observations

- Monthly sales generally increase throughout the year.
- November and December show the highest sales values in the dataset.
- Laptop has the highest product sales.
- Mouse has the lowest product sales.
- Monthly profit generally follows the upward trend in sales.
- Order volume also increases toward the end of the year.
- The scatter plot shows a positive relationship between sales and profit in this practice dataset.

> Note: This project uses a practice dataset created for learning purposes and does not represent real-world business data.
